2.3Assessment Methodology

  • Code enumeration. {{STACK_AND_ENTRY_POINTS}}
  • Dependency and secret checks. {{DEPENDENCY_AND_SECRET_CHECKS_AND_TOOLS_RUN}}
  • Manual source analysis. Followed each entry point (routes and handlers, screens and client flows) through its middleware, filters and guards to its sinks, with a STRIDE pass on every trust boundary, against the checklist in section 4.0 and the stack checks for {{STACK}}.
  • Reporting. Each finding lists affected lines, its standard references, a CVSS 3.1 score, impact, a fix that uses the codebase's existing helpers, and a proof of concept.

2.7Risk Criteria

Each finding is rated by its CVSS 3.1 base score. Where log or repository access is a precondition, the score assumes low-privileged readers (PR:L).

RiskCVSS 3.1Description
Critical9.0 – 10.0Exposed and exploitable with severe impact; no control is in place or planned.
High7.0 – 8.9High concern given exposure, ease of exploitation or severity of impact.
Medium4.0 – 6.9Moderate concern; a control exists but is partial or only somewhat effective.
Low0.1 – 3.9Minor concern; the control is in place but could be improved.
Informational0.0Hardening advice with no direct exploit path today.
